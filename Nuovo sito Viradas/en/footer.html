Legal information

This information explains, clearly and transparently, the nature of the services offered through Viradas, how personal data voluntarily provided by visitors is handled, and how the website uses technical tools.

1. Professional disclaimer

The counseling offered through Viradas falls within the fields of personal support, prevention and personal development.

It is not psychotherapy, it is not a healthcare profession, it does not provide diagnoses or medical or psychological treatment, and it does not replace therapy or psychiatric care.

The service is intended for adults and focuses on awareness, personal resources, managing emotions, boundaries, relationships and processes of change.

If you experience significant symptoms, risks to yourself or others, or circumstances requiring clinical assessment, please consult a doctor, psychologist or psychotherapist.

2. Personal data processing (Privacy)

Data provided through the contact form (name, email, optional phone number and message content) is used solely to respond to your enquiry.

Data is not sold or shared with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

The form service provider and website hosting provider may process data as service providers.

You may request correction or deletion of your personal data by writing to: info@viradas.com.

3. Cookies and analytics tools

This website uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect statistical information about how the site is used (such as pages visited, visit duration, device type and browser). IP address anonymisation is enabled in Google Analytics, while Google Signals and ad personalisation features are disabled to reduce the identifiability of visitors. Google processes the data to generate aggregated reports on website traffic and interactions, used solely to improve the content and functioning of the pages. You can restrict or block Google Analytics cookies in your browser settings or, where available, through the preferences in the website’s cookie banner.

4. Security

The website uses a secure HTTPS connection. Information submitted through the forms is not stored locally by the website: messages are transmitted through the form submission service (Formspree).

5. Your rights

You may request at any time:

  • access to the data you submitted;
  • correction of inaccurate information;
  • deletion of your data;
  • restriction of data processing.

Simply write to: info@viradas.com.
