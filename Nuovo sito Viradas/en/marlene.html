About me

Marlene Masino.

Counsellor SGfB · Lugano and online

My background

Relationships, body, movement.

I support individuals and groups at times when life feels constricted, or is experienced that way.

Viradas grew out of working with people and in situations where freedom of choice can feel limited: a space to notice what holds us back and explore new possibilities.

My work brings together dialogue, body awareness and personal resources, while respecting each person’s pace and needs.

My experience

After my first post-university job in marketing, training in counseling and a volunteering experience in 2009 led me towards work centred on supporting others.

I have worked with teenagers in Brazil, unemployed people and, for more than 17 years, people dealing with the consequences of criminal proceedings.

What guides my work

Freedom of choice and movement, enjoyment and wellbeing in relationships, mindful communication.
