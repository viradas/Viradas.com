My journey

One Turning Point That Opened the Way for Others.

I support individuals and groups at times when life feels restrictive, whether because of their circumstances or how they experience them.

After spending the first years of my career in online marketing in the banking sector, I changed direction too. Training in counseling and a volunteering experience in Brazil in 2009 firmly set my professional path towards supporting others.

Since then, I have worked with people who, for different reasons, face situations where their choices are limited and making changes can be difficult.

In my work, I combine dialogue with attention to lifestyle and the body, as additional ways of communicating that can help facilitate change.

My experience

I have gained experience in settings where people's choices are often limited:

  • With children and teenagers experiencing marginalisation and violence, in Brazil (three months).
  • With people looking for employment, for six and a half years.
  • With people dealing with the consequences of criminal proceedings, for over 17 years.
Mission, vision and values

Mission

To contribute to people's well-being by supporting those who feel under pressure in recognising who they are, what they want and what practical steps they can take towards choices and relationships that bring pleasure and satisfaction.

Vision

Relationships and living environments where people can move freely, without restrictions or destructive dynamics, while staying true to themselves in the choices they make.

Values

  • Freedom of choice and movement
  • Pleasure and well-being in relationships
  • Conscious communication

Beyond work

A more personal note.

If I had a magic wand, I would live several lives at once: I would explore every corner of the Earth and a little of outer space, learn different trades, develop more talents, make distances relative and abolish time.

In this life, I chase travels, concerts and experiences. I learn languages and other forms of expression, musical instruments and disciplines for body and mind: anything that helps me understand.
I appreciate the encounters life brings and let myself be drawn to those that show promise of becoming deep connections.

I try to turn desires into choices, dreams into projects and burdens into freedom.

Partly for myself, partly for those like me.
